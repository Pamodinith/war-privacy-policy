Privacy Policy

WAR is a WhatsApp automation application used to respond to messages sent to the connected WhatsApp business account.

Information We Process

When you contact the connected WhatsApp account, the application may receive your WhatsApp phone number, message content, message type, and basic message metadata provided through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

How We Use Information

This information is used only to receive messages, identify the conversation, provide automated replies, and operate the requested WhatsApp service.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Messages are transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform as part of providing the messaging service.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as needed to operate the service. To request deletion of information associated with your interaction, contact the service owner using the contact information provided with the WhatsApp account.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the application. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the owner of the connected WhatsApp service.
